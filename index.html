👋About

I am a second-year Ph.D. student in the Department of Data Science at the New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT), where I am advised by Prof. Mengjia Xu in the Xu Lab. My research is on the geometry of large language models: how their hidden states are organized, and how that structure can be used at inference time. Recent work includes activation steering guided by the model's own uncertainty, hyperbolic representation learning for LLMs, and LLM reasoning over mathematical and tabular problems.

Feel free to reach out by email if you would like to chat or collaborate.
